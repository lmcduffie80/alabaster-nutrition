## Unlisted distribution

After the app is approved, or while it's in review, submit Apple's request form from
<https://developer.apple.com/support/unlisted-app-distribution/>. Say the app is a business tool shared only with
invited businesses and their staff. Once Apple accepts the request, the app gets a link that works for installs
but doesn't appear in search.
